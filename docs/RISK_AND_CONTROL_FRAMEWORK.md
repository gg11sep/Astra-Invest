# Astra Risk & Control Framework
**Version 1.1 – November 2026**

## Hard Position Limits

| Constraint                     | Mild     | Moderate | Severe   |
|--------------------------------|----------|----------|----------|
| Max Single Stock               | 8–10%   | 5–7%    | 3–5%    |
| Max High-Circularity AI        | 12–15%  | 6–8%    | 0–3%    |
| Min Cash + Short HQ Bonds      | 15%     | 30%     | 45%+    |
| Gold Core Allocation           | 8–12%   | 10–15%  | 15–20%  |

## Autonomy Levels
- Level 0: Research only
- Level 1: Advisory (current default)
- Level 2: Paper portfolio
- Level 3: Live execution (future – requires separate governance)

## Mandatory Logging
Every size-guided recommendation must log:
- Timestamp
- Regime + Stress Score
- Key inputs
- Agents involved
- Size guidance + rationale
- Invalidation conditions
